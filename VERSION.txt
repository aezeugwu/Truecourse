TrueCourse v0.11.0
Built: 2026-10-02

ALIGNMENT: all ten steps of "How to Talk to the Universe", as daily practice.
The app does not claim the book's explanation is true. It records the
practices and shows, from YOUR history, whether days with them look different.

WHERE EACH STEP LIVES
 1 Limiting beliefs ........ Practice → Alignment → Beliefs I'm rewriting
 2 Replacement beliefs ..... same; shows on Practice → Morning and Evening
                             with an "I read it" tap, and on Home
 3 What + Why + Feeling .... Practice → Alignment → Your intention (also on
                             Home). Joins into one sentence.
 4 Gratitude anchoring ..... Practice → Alignment → Morning alignment
                             (3 gratitudes + 1 thing on its way; Quick mode)
 5 Future-self decisions ... Log → Pattern / Incident → detail section
 6 Visualization ........... Alignment → 5-minute timer (Quick = 2 min); ends
                             by showing your obstacle and plan
 7 Curate environment ...... Review → What's draining you + Environment audit
                             (once per cycle; Home reminds you until done)
 8 Anchors ................. Home → Your intention: sentence + replacement
                             belief, "Breathe & reconnect" (30 s), and
                             "Lock-screen picture" (saves a phone-sized image)
 9 Body baseline ........... Alignment → four taps; Review compares with Day Score
10 Detachment / reset ...... Pattern log "What is this asking me to adjust?"
                             + 2-minute reset (Home missed-block banner, Alignment)

THE FULL 10 STEPS are the last entry in the Guide, with a button to Alignment;
Alignment has a button back to them.

REVIEW (new cards): Alignment practice, Body baseline (each compared with
your Day Score), What's draining you, Environment audit.
Those comparisons now use FINISHED days only. Before, today was counted,
which skewed the Practice comparison too. That one is fixed as well.
Honest limits: a comparison needs days in both groups; it shows "not enough
days" until then, and says it is your history, not proof of cause.

OTHER
- Journal shows your alignment practice and the two new Pattern lines.
- Backup/restore includes beliefs and audits.
- Everything fills in for a missed day with the ◀ ▶ bar, like the rest of Practice.
- Timer counts from the clock, so a locked screen doesn't throw it off.
  "Stop early" does not count as done.

NOT BUILT: appointment blocks that flag overlapping work blocks.
