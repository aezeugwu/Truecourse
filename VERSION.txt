VALUE AUDIT IS NOW REAL (the last place that showed sample numbers)

What was wrong: Review's Value Audit looked like your data and wasn't. The 12
bar widths came from a formula on each pair's number (40 + (id x 7) mod 40),
and the line "Where your B-time went: Reactive (9) - Consuming (6) - Drift (4)"
was typed into the code. The Guide admitted the bars were illustrative; the
typed line wasn't flagged anywhere.

Now, for the scope you pick (Day, Week, Cycle, Lunar, All):
- Each pair you've tagged gets a bar: how your logged blocks split between the
  A side and the B side, from what you tapped under "what it actually was".
- Under each bar: how many blocks and how many hours went to each side.
- A coverage line says how many sessions were tagged out of how many you
  logged. Blocks you didn't tag aren't counted.
- Pairs with no tags aren't drawn as bars; they're listed as "not tagged".
- A sum line ties it to your headline number: "All pairs together: 5 of 9
  tags are A-side - that's your Execution Ratio (56%)". The bars are by number
  of tagged blocks, the same basis as the Execution Ratio, so they always agree.
- "Where your B-time went" now lists your three biggest B sides by real hours.
- With nothing tagged yet, it says so instead of showing bars.

The Guide's Review entry now explains this instead of admitting the bars were
illustrative.
