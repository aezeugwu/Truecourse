# TrueCourse v0.19.0

*Observe → Capture → Learn → Change*

A personal cycle and execution app, the successor to PC-EOS and 60D-POS. It runs
on your phone as an installable web app, keeps everything on that phone (no
account, no server), and is built to keep working without a connection after the first load.

Version 0.19.0 · built 2026-10-03 · the version number is shown at the bottom
of **Settings**.

## Rules worth knowing

- **Saving.** Practice, Projects and most fields save the instant you type or tap. There is no Save step to forget. (Block Activity and Pattern entries save when you tap their button.)
- **Filling in a missed day.** The ◀ ▶ date bar works in **Practice**, **Log → Block Activity** and **Log → Pattern / Incident**. **Projects → Mark progress** and **Incubator** take a date. Practice, Block Activity and Pattern entries filled in later are marked "logged later". You can never log into the future.
- **A new day.** If the app stays open past midnight, it refreshes itself when you return to it. Nothing is lost.
- **Choosing your hours.** Settings → Day hours sets when the day starts, how long Critical and Development blocks last, and when Evening Practice starts. To change one day, tap it in Cycle. To change today, use **Edit times / projects ›** on Home. Blocks are always listed in time order.
- **History is never rewritten.** Anything you've logged or marked done keeps the time it was logged at, whatever you change later.
- **Progress control.** − and + move it 5% a tap, so a stray touch can't jump it to 100%. Tap the number to type an exact value. The "last moved" clock only restarts when progress goes *up*.
- **Your Cycle Commitment.** Mark your ONE priority "Abandoned" on 3 or more days in a cycle and Home shows your stake back to you until you acknowledge it. Nothing is charged or locked.
- **Breakfast, lunch and appointments.** Breakfast and lunch are *windows* (a from-time and a to-time, Settings → Protected times); nothing is scheduled inside them. A work block that would land in one moves, whole, to just after it, and the time before stays free; a block is never cut in half. **Appointments** (Settings → Appointments) are one-time, can't-move times, such as a meeting. On its day it shows on Home as a locked block and overrides whatever was there: work moves to just after it, noted "moved for: …". Once it has passed it disappears from Home (the work it displaced stays where it moved). Switch it off and everything returns. Anything you've logged, and any block you set by hand, is never moved; a hand-set block that sits on one of these is flagged "overlaps …" (a logged block is history, so it is never flagged). If nothing fits before Evening Practice, Home says "Doesn't fit today: …" and names the blocks instead of squeezing or hiding them. A quiet note appears if a day's work blocks add up to more than 10 hours (never a limit). Change one day in Cycle → tap the day.
- **Session handoffs.** At the end of a block (Log → Block Activity, card "Leave a runway for next time") you leave two things: **Current state** (where exactly you stopped) and **▶ Next action** (the first thing to do on return). "+ full handoff" adds the target, starting point, where to continue (file, version, link; pre-filled from last time) and a blocker or decision needed. The AIs you used come from the project, already ticked at the top of the log. What you wrote under "What did you actually do?" counts as what you completed. A handoff belongs to the **project**, so it keeps its history as the project moves between Critical, Development, Sleeping and Done. You see it on Home ("▶ Pick up: …", and "Leave your handoff ›" in the last 5 minutes of a work block), in Block Activity ("Last time on …"), in Projects (latest and full history) and in the Journal. **Copy for another AI** (in Projects) copies PROJECT, OBJECTIVE, STATE, NEXT ACTION, AUTHORITATIVE FILE, WORKSPACE, BLOCKER, DO NOT REDO and LAST SESSION, ready to paste into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude. If you leave no next action on today's block, the first tap on Save shows a reminder instead of saving and a second tap saves without one (filling in a past day is never nudged). Removing a project deletes its handoffs (it says so first); **Mark complete** keeps them.
- **Cycle colors.** Each past day is colored from its scheduled work blocks, scored with the same Day Score as Home and Review (completed = 1, partial = ½, not done = 0): **green Full** 70% or more, **gold Minimum viable** 30–69%, **red Disrupted** under 30%, **pale grey** for no record (the app wasn't opened) or a rest day, never red. White is today and the future. Tap a past day to see its score and each block. The 70% and 30% cut-offs are fixed.
- **Value Audit (Review).** For each of the 12 pairs you've tagged, a bar shows how your logged blocks split between the A side and the B side, with the number of blocks and hours under each side. It counts what you tapped under "what it actually was" when you logged a block; blocks you didn't tag aren't counted, and the card says how many that was. Bars are by number of tagged blocks, so all pairs together add up to your Execution Ratio (the card shows the sum). "Where your B-time went" lists your three biggest B sides by hours.
- **Lunar calendar and power days.** Settings → **Your sign**: enter your birth date and the app works out your sun sign from where the Sun really was (a sign-change birthday is flagged, and you can choose your sign). The Lunar tab then marks **★ Primary** days (the Moon is in your sign) and **◆ Secondary** days (the Moon is in another sign of your element) on the real lunar cycle; a day counts if the Moon is in that sign at any time during it. Tap a day for its phase, what it is believed to favour and avoid, and the exact hours the Moon is in each sign. The Moon's and Sun's positions are calculated, not looked up: phases and sign changes are accurate to about an hour (New and Full Moon times to within a few minutes). **Everything about influence is stated as a belief** ("It is believed that…") and is never imposed; whether to go by it is up to you. Home shows the true phase and a small tag on a power day. Your birth date stays on the phone and is part of your backup.
- **AIs and milestones, entered once per project.** In Projects → open a project: tick the AIs you work with there (the seven defaults, plus any you add, which then join the list for every project) and optionally note where each one lives (its project or chat name). Add milestones one at a time or paste a list with one per line; ▲ ▼ reorder them, and the first one not yet reached is the **next milestone**. Your old single "next milestone" box becomes milestone one automatically. In the log, the project's AIs appear already ticked (untick any you didn't use today; "+ other AIs" adds one for that session) and the next milestone shows with a "✓ Reached — next ›" button and an undo.
- **Drafts.** Everything you type in Log → Block Activity is saved on the phone as you type, one draft per block and day. Switch tabs to look something up, reload, or close the app: it is all still there, marked 📝 draft on Home and in the block list, and Log reopens on it. Nothing is *recorded* until you tap Save, which clears the draft; "Discard draft" throws it away (it asks first). Drafts are part of your backup.
- **Compress remaining schedule** (Home, after missed blocks) saves its new times, so a later change can't undo it.
- **Timers end with a soft chime** (visualization, 2-minute reset, 30-second reconnect), so you know without opening your eyes. It plays at your phone's *media* volume. The screen stays awake while a timer runs, because a phone whose screen has switched off can't sound anything. "Stop early" is silent. Settings → Timers has an on/off switch and **Play a sample**; play it before you close your eyes.

## Alignment: the ten steps

Built from your summary of *How to Talk to the Universe*. The app does **not**
claim the book's explanation is true. It records the practices and shows, from
your own history, whether days with them look different.

| # | Step | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Limiting beliefs | Practice → Alignment → *Beliefs I'm rewriting* → + Add a belief |
| 2 | Replacement beliefs | Same form. Shows on Practice → Morning and Evening with **I read it**, and on Home |
| 3 | What + Why + Feeling | Practice → Alignment → *Your intentions* (the one in focus is also on Home) |
| 4 | Gratitude | Practice → Alignment → *Morning alignment* (3 gratitudes + one thing on its way; Quick mode) |
| 5 | Future-self decisions | Log → Pattern / Incident → + why / effect / link to project |
| 6 | Visualization | Alignment → Start 5-minute visualization (Quick = 2 min); ends by showing your obstacle and plan |
| 7 | Curate your environment | Review → *What's draining you* and *Environment audit* (once per cycle; Home reminds you) |
| 8 | Anchors | Home → *Your intention*: **Breathe & reconnect** (30 s) and **Lock-screen picture** |
| 9 | Body baseline | Alignment → four taps; Review compares with your Day Score |
| 10 | Detachment and reset | Pattern log "What is this asking me to adjust?" and the **2-minute reset** (Home banner, Alignment → Tools) |

### Up to three intentions

You can hold up to **3 active intentions** at once, each with What, Why, Feeling,
Outcome, Obstacle, Plan and optional linked projects (one intention can serve several projects, and several intentions can share one). One is **in focus**: it is
what Home, the lock-screen picture, Breathe & reconnect and the visualization's
obstacle-and-plan reminder use. Home shows "2 of 3" with a **Next intention ›**
tap. Manage them in Practice → Alignment → *Your intentions*: add, edit, switch
focus, mark achieved, remove. Achieved ones move to a list with the date and free
a slot.

**The obstacle check.** When an obstacle actually happens, log it in Log →
Pattern / Incident → *+ why / effect / link to project*, choose which intention it
was the obstacle for, and answer "Did I run my plan?" (Yes / Partly / No). Review →
*Intentions* then shows, for each one, how often the obstacle hit and how often you
ran your plan. It counts only entries you linked and never guesses from the words
you typed.

**Crediting work.** When you log time in Block Activity on a linked project, it shows
which intention it serves and your plan. If one intention serves the project, the
session counts toward it automatically. If two or more share it, you can tick which
one(s) the session advanced. Review → *Intentions* then shows sessions and hours
credited per intention, plus each linked project's progress. A session ticked for
more than one intention counts in full for each, so those totals overlap; hours are
not split. Progress is per project, so intentions sharing a project show the same
percentage. Each project's detail in Projects shows which intentions it serves, and
deleting a project removes it from every intention. Work is credited from the day
you installed this update; earlier sessions are not.

Review compares your Day Score on days you did a practice against days you
didn't, **using finished days only**. It shows "not enough days yet" until both
kinds exist, and it labels the result as your own history, not proof of cause.
The lock-screen picture is saved to your Downloads; you set it as wallpaper
yourself. Timers count from the clock and end with a soft chime plus a vibration.

## Your data and backup

Everything is stored in this phone's browser storage. Clearing the browser's
site data **deletes it**, so export a backup first.

**Settings → Backup → Export JSON** saves ONE file with everything the app keeps on
the phone: templates and weekly defaults, day plans (including per-block times),
project progress and last-progress dates, projects, ideas, triggers, your
intentions (and which one is in focus), beliefs, accountability, logs, practice
entries, day hours, protected times, appointments, environment audits, **each past
day's schedule record** (which blocks were Completed or Partial), **your cycle start
date**, your Google Client ID, your Timers and Alerts switches, your **session handoffs**, and the **Done shelf**.

**Import JSON** puts all of it back and tells you what came back, for example
"Imported successfully: 14 days of schedules, 63 log entries, 3 intentions." A
file that is not a TrueCourse backup is refused. Days in the file replace the same
days on the phone; days that are not in the file are left alone. Backups made by
older versions still restore (they simply don't contain the newer items).

## Updating the app

Each release changes **three files**: `app.js`, `sw.js`, `VERSION.txt`.

1. In your GitHub repository, upload those three over the old ones (same names).
2. Commit, wait about a minute for GitHub Pages to redeploy.
3. On your phone, fully close TrueCourse (recent apps → swipe it away) and reopen it. Do it twice if the first time doesn't show the change.
4. Check **Settings → bottom of the page**. It must show the new version number. If it shows an older one, your phone is still running a cached copy.

Never clear site data to force an update; it erases your data. Export first.
`sw.js` must change in every release (its version label is what tells the phone
there is something new), which is why it is always one of the three.

## First-time install

1. Create a public GitHub repository.
2. Upload **all 9 files** in this folder to the repository root, loose, not zipped.
3. Settings → Pages → Source: your main branch, root folder → Save.
4. Open the GitHub Pages address on your phone in Chrome.
5. Chrome menu → Add to Home screen.

## Known gaps (not done, or not working yet)

**Not built**
- Splitting a work block to fit around a window: blocks always move whole. Two blocks you set by hand can still share a time; only collisions with protected times and appointments are flagged.
- A Discard action for Incubator ideas.
- Backfill on Lunar (it has nothing to enter).
- A migration tool from PC-EOS / 60D-POS data.
- Syncing between devices.
- Two-way calendar sync.

**Unverified**
- **The timer chime** was checked by measuring the sound the app generates (quiet, slow swell, slow fade), not by listening on a real phone. It plays at media volume, and keeping the screen awake depends on the phone and browser supporting it.
- **Offline use.** The app's files are cached on the phone, so it should open without a connection. That has not been tested on a real phone.
- **Google Calendar sync** is a one-way push of today's Critical and Development blocks. It has never been tested against a real Google account (it needs a Client ID; steps are in the Guide). It only syncs while the app is open.

**Minor**
- If the app wasn't opened on a past day, that day's schedule is rebuilt from your templates and hours as they are now.
