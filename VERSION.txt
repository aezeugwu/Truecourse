TIMERS NOW END WITH A SOFT CHIME

Before, a timer ended with a vibration only, which does nothing if the
phone is face down, and nothing at all if the screen has switched off.
That is a problem when your eyes are closed.

- Three quiet, bell-like notes (C, E, G) that swell in gently and fade over
  about three seconds. Made on the phone, no sound file.
- Plays when the 5-minute / 2-minute visualization, the 2-minute reset and the
  30-second reconnect finish. "Stop early" stays silent.
- The screen is kept awake while a timer counts down, because a phone whose
  screen has switched off cannot sound anything. It is released the moment
  the timer ends or you close it.
- The sound is unlocked when you tap Start (phones only allow sound after a
  tap), so it is allowed to play minutes later.
- Settings → Timers: a switch to turn the chime off, and "Play a sample" so
  you can set your volume BEFORE you close your eyes.

IT PLAYS AT YOUR PHONE'S MEDIA VOLUME. If you hear nothing, turn media volume up.

NOT BUILT (offered, not requested): a second, quieter chime if the timer
screen is still open 30 seconds after the first.
