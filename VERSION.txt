TrueCourse v0.7.2
Built: 2026-09-25

Two things addressed.

1. Confirmed by design, not a bug: Morning and Afternoon have no Save
   button because every field there auto-saves the instant you tap or
   type — same pattern as most of the app. Evening keeps its Save
   button as a closing confirmation, since it's the one screen with
   several fields you might fill in over a few minutes.

2. Real crash, found and reproduced: Log would go completely blank
   with no error shown if you had zero Critical and zero Development
   projects (very reachable after testing the remove-project feature)
   — the screen tried to default to "the first available project" and
   crashed when there wasn't one. Fixed with a proper message instead
   ("No Critical or Development projects yet — add one in Projects, or
   promote an idea from the Incubator"). Also hardened the same screen
   against a second, rarer empty-schedule edge case found while fixing
   the first one.

Both reproduced and confirmed fixed with the exact broken state
recreated first, not just read through the code.
