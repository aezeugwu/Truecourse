DELEGATING WORK: HAND IT OFF, FREE THE SLOT, STILL NEVER FORGET IT

THE PROBLEM
A Critical project that once took two and a half hours now just needs a quick
check on the agent doing it. It should stop holding a Critical slot, and still be
tracked, with many such projects handled from ONE time-box.

WHAT YOU GET
1. AGENTS (Projects -> Agents). Unlimited. Name, what they do, email, WhatsApp,
   phone, notes. Call / WhatsApp / Email open your phone's own app with the message
   ready. A number typed locally (0801 234 5678) becomes international using the
   default country code in Settings -> Delegation (+234 to start). Contact details
   are part of your backup.
2. DISPATCH. Open a Critical, Development or Sleeping project -> "Dispatch to
   agents...". Pick the agents (add one on the spot), give each their own part, set
   a report-back date and how often to check in (3 days to start). The brief is
   written from the project: name, objective, why it matters, milestones in order.
   The project moves to DELEGATED (a new tab, no limit) and its slot is free at once.
   You are offered each agent's brief to send, and can promote another project into
   the freed Critical slot with one tap. Milestones, AIs, handoffs and progress
   travel with it.
3. THE DELEGATION DESK. The first dispatch creates it. It takes ONE Development slot
   and holds every delegated project. Give it a Development time block like any
   project: choose "Delegation Desk" when you log that block and you see all your
   delegated projects, most urgent first. Open one to contact the agent, move its
   milestone, set its status (Dispatched / In progress / Needs my answer / Blocked /
   Delivered), record questions in both directions with the answers, and log a
   check-in (saved as you type). "Write this from the check-ins I logged today" fills
   in your block note.
4. NOT FORGETTING. Each delegated project has its own check-in clock (instead of the
   14-day stagnation flag). Logging a check-in schedules the next. HOME shows
   "Check-ins due" for anything overdue, due today, with a question waiting from the
   agent, blocked, or delivered. Review's project line counts delegated work.
5. WHEN IT COMES BACK. "Accept & close" sends a finished project to Done with its
   whole history (including who had it). "Take back" returns it to Critical or
   Development when a slot is free.

HONEST LIMITS
- The app cannot send messages or see replies by itself (it has no server). It opens
  WhatsApp / email / the dialer with the text ready; you record what happened.
- It does not notify you when an agent finishes. The check-in clock and Home's
  "Check-ins due" are how you are reminded.
- The Desk uses one of your six Development slots. If all six are full, free one
  before the first dispatch (the panel says so).

FOUND AND FIXED WHILE TESTING
- A helper clamped negative day counts to zero, which would have shown every
  delegated project as "due today" forever. Replaced with a signed count.
