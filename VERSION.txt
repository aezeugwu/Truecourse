TrueCourse v0.8.1
Built: 2026-09-29

Fixed: the footer at the bottom of Settings said "v0.3 — draft preview"
through every release since v0.3, so nothing in the app could tell you
which version your phone was actually running. It now shows the real
version, read from a single value in the code.

Also added a pre-release check that compares the version shown in the
app, the cache name in sw.js, VERSION.txt and README.md, and refuses to
ship if any of them disagree. A stale sw.js cache name is exactly what
kept your phone on old versions earlier, so it can't be left to memory.

To confirm you're on this version: Settings → scroll to the bottom →
it should read "TrueCourse v0.8.1".
