THE THREE PROBLEMS YOU SHOWED IN YOUR RECORDING

1. AIs, entered once per project
   Projects -> open a project -> "AIs I work with on this project": tick Claude,
   ChatGPT, Copilot, Qwen, Perplexity, Gemini, Grok. "+ Add another AI" adds one
   to the list for every project. Under each ticked AI, optionally note where it
   lives (its project or chat name). In Log -> Block Activity the project's AIs
   appear ALREADY TICKED at the top; untick any you did not use today, or use
   "+ other AIs" for one-off ones. The fixed ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini /
   Perplexity / Other buttons and the "AI project or chat" box are gone: that
   information now lives with the project and is entered once. An AI you added
   cannot be removed from the list while a project still uses it (it says which).

2. Milestones where you work
   Projects: a milestone LIST per project. Type them, or paste a list (one per
   line). The first one not yet reached is the NEXT MILESTONE. Tick one when
   reached; reorder with the arrows. Your old single "next milestone" box counts
   as item one, so nothing you typed is lost.
   Log -> Block Activity: right under the project, a "project at a glance" card:
   next milestone (for example "2 of 5") with "Reached - next" and "Undo last
   reached", the objective, where you left off, and the AIs. The closed project
   card in Projects shows the next milestone and the AIs too.

3. Losing what you typed
   Cause: the log form lived only on screen, so leaving the Log tab threw it away.
   Now everything in Block Activity autosaves as you type, one draft per block and
   day. Switch tabs, reload, or close the app: it is all still there and Log
   reopens on your draft. A block with a draft is marked "draft" on Home and in the
   block list; several drafts can wait at once ("Other drafts" jumps between them).
   NOTHING IS RECORDED until you tap Save, which clears that draft. "Discard
   draft" (it asks first) throws one away. Just opening the log, or typing and then
   clearing it again, leaves no draft. Drafts are included in your backup.
   To your question: you do not need to press Save until you are finished, and you
   can leave and come back to continue.

ALSO
- Moving a project between Critical, Development, Sleeping and Done keeps its AIs
  and milestones.
- "Copy for another AI" shows the current next milestone, and the AIs with where each
  lives.
- The Guide's Projects, Log and Session handoff entries explain all of this.
