LUNAR CALENDAR AND POWER DAYS

THE LUNAR TAB is now a tappable calendar of one lunar cycle (the day of one New
Moon up to the day before the next: 29 or 30 days), with the real moon phase on
every day. The arrows step between cycles.

POWER DAYS. Settings -> Your sign: enter your birth date. The app works out your
sun sign from where the Sun really was that day. If you were born on a day the
Sun changed signs, it says so and lets you choose. Then, on the Lunar tab:
  * Primary power day: the Moon is in your sign
  * Secondary harmony day: the Moon is in another sign of your element
A day is marked if the Moon is in that sign at any time during it.

TAP A DAY for: its phase and the guidance you supplied, whether it is a power
day for you and why, the exact moment of a New / First Quarter / Full / Last
Quarter Moon when one falls that day, and the hours the Moon is in each sign.

BELIEFS ARE STATED AS BELIEFS. Everything about influence reads "It is believed
that..." and is never imposed. The app only calculates where the Moon really is.

WHY THE DATES COME FROM A CALCULATION, NOT FROM THE DOCUMENT
The power-day text you supplied lists one month of Moon-in-sign dates. They do
not match the real sky in any of the Octobers 2024 to 2027 checked (0 of 13
windows within a day), and the Moon changes sign every 2-3 days, so the dates
shift every month. Its wording (the themes and descriptions) is used; its dates
are not.

ACCURACY. The Moon's and Sun's positions are calculated (Meeus series) and were
checked against published data: eclipse positions within 0.74 degrees, New Moon
times within a minute, Full Moons within a few minutes, the March 2026 equinox
within ten minutes. Phases and sign changes are accurate to about an hour.

THE PHASE IS NOW THE REAL PHASE. The old average-month model could be up to 17
hours out (for example it put the 10 Oct 2026 New Moon on the 11th), which could
show the wrong phase on a day around a change. Home and the Lunar tab now agree,
and "Lunar Day n/29" now says 29 or 30 as the real cycle is.

Your birth date stays on this phone. It is included in your backup file.
